Introduction

Mahendra Singh Dhoni (MS Dhoni) is one of India's greatest cricketers and one of the most successful captains in cricket history. He is widely known as "Captain Cool" because of his calm and composed leadership style.

International Carrier

Made hid ODI debut against Bangladesh in Decemeber 2004. Scored 148 against Pakistan in only his 5th ODI, announcing himself on the international stage. Became the fastest player to reach No. 1 in the ICC ODI batting rankings

Captaincy Carrier

MS Dhoni captained India in 332 international matches, the most by any captain in international cricket. He won 178 matches during his tenure. 🏆 ICC T20 World Cup 2007 – Winner 🏆 ICC Cricket World Cup 2011 – Winner 🏆 ICC Champions Trophy 2013 – Winner

Batting Records

**MS Dhoni's Batting Records** MS Dhoni is one of the greatest wicketkeeper-batsmen in cricket history. He scored **17,266 international runs** across Tests, ODIs, and T20Is. In ODIs, he accumulated **10,773 runs** at an impressive average of **50.57**. His highest ODI score was **183* against Sri Lanka in 2005**, one of the highest scores by a wicketkeeper. Dhoni played a match-winning innings of **91* in the 2011 World Cup Final**, helping India win the trophy. He was famous for his finishing ability, powerful sixes, and the iconic **Helicopter Shot**.

Why He Is Called "Captain Cool"

Dhoni became famous for staying calm under pressure and making bold decisions, such as backing young players and taking responsibility in crucial matches, including the 2011 World Cup final where he scored an unbeaten 91.

Legacy

MS Dhoni is widely regarded as one of the greatest captains and finishers in cricket history. He transformed Indian cricket with his calm leadership and strong decision-making under pressure. Dhoni is the only captain to win all three ICC white-ball trophies: the **2007 T20 World Cup, 2011 ODI World Cup, and 2013 Champions Trophy**. He played a key role in developing young talents like Virat Kohli, Rohit Sharma, and Ravindra Jadeja. His leadership of Chennai Super Kings made the franchise one of the most successful in IPL history. Dhoni’s legacy is defined by his “Captain Cool” mindset, finishing ability, and lasting impact on Indian cricket.
